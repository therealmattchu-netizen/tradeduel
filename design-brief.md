# TradeDuel Design Brief

- Design read: A competitive trading arena for crypto-native players who want skill-based head-to-head trading challenges without wagers or custody.
- Concept spine: The arena reveal. A single crossed-swords emblem emerges from a dark metal arena as visitors move through the story.
- Delivery tier: cinema, built around a scroll-controlled continuous hero film with restrained chapter transitions.
- Locked palette: graphite `#111116`, deep charcoal `#191820`, violet `#A66BFF`, soft white `#F4F1FA`, muted grey `#9B97A8`. The violet matches the established logo and teaser; no secondary accent colors.
- Locked type: Satoshi display + JetBrains Mono for technical labels and data.
- Animation mode: animated-website
- Journey shape: single-shot. The brand story is one subject seen more closely, not separate worlds.
- Journey: (1) The market is your arena. A distant crossed-swords emblem emerges from darkness. (2) Every duel has rules. The emblem grows as the page explains wallet-owned trades and time-limited matches. (3) Skill, measured. The camera resolves etched market geometry while explaining transparent scoring and ELO. (4) Watch the matchup. The arena becomes a setting for a product preview, clearly marked as illustrative rather than live. (5) Enter when ready. The emblem settles into its final centered beauty state with a coming-soon CTA.
- World grammar: near-black graphite arena, brushed dark metal, restrained violet rim lighting, centered crossed-swords subject, gentle continuous push-in and slight orbit, low-detail negative space for copy.
- Mobile framing: emblem and focal details remain centered, copy anchors low and left, nav becomes horizontally scrollable, no essential content depends on motion.
- Section plan: cinematic scroll journey; asymmetric product preview; two-column how-it-works; horizontal integrity principles; final high-contrast launch CTA.
- Asset plan: generated arena hero, dark material plate, duel concept visual, one continuous 15-second hero motion derived from the hero art, exact-frame poster, favicon and social metadata. User's crossed-swords brand mark represented as a clean inline SVG where exact source artwork is unavailable.
- CTA inventory: primary `Explore the arena` scrolls to product preview; secondary `How it works` scrolls to explanation; final `Follow the launch` is a non-fabricated external X placeholder until the user supplies a verified handle.
- Trust constraint: do not claim wallet authentication, live chain indexing, match creation, leaderboards, user counts, or ELO is live until implemented and tested. Use explicit coming-soon labels. No wagers, custody, guaranteed returns, fake stats, or fake player activity.
